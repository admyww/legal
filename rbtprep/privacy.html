We do not collect your data

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account to create. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising, and the app does not send your study activity anywhere.

Nothing leaves your device. Everything the app remembers is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

  • Which questions you have answered
  • Which questions you answered incorrectly
  • Which questions you saved
  • Your session history — date, time, score, and number of questions

Deleting the app removes all of it. We have no copy and no way to recover it.
