Common questions

Is this the official BACB exam?
No. This is an independent study aid. The official examination is administered by the Behavior Analyst Certification Board.

My progress disappeared.
Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, and we have no backup.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Every question is included in the app.
